## 2025-05-18 - Tooltip Accessibility for Icon-only Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons relying solely on `aria-label` are accessible to screen readers, but do not provide visual hints (like titles/tooltips) for sighted users or mouse users hovering over them, which diminishes overall UX. When implementing Radix UI tooltips, adding multiple localized `<TooltipProvider>` wrappers breaks the global hover delay synchronization.
**Action:** Always wrap icon-only buttons with Radix UI's `<Tooltip>` and `<TooltipContent>` components (with `<TooltipTrigger asChild>` mapping to the `<Button>`) to ensure both screen reader support and visual hover context. To preserve global hover delays and prevent component test failures, include exactly one `<TooltipProvider>` at the root layout (e.g., `layout.tsx`) and within testing wrappers, rather than inside individual components.
